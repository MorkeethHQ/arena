# Poster lane stock pack

**Clock:** 12 minutes  
**Tiebreak:** timeline hook

## Sealed task

Build one offline HTML poster with a fixed 1200×630 CSS-pixel artboard for a
fictional personal-AI dual meet titled “Assistants Go to War.” Show two invented
fighter names, a round or clock mark, and one roast strip. A small designer
credit may name the fighter, but the deliverable must read as a fight poster.

## Rubric

- The declared file exists and opens; its root artboard is exactly 1200×630.
- The title, two invented fighter names, and round/clock mark are readable.
- One mean-line roast strip is present.
- No external network assets, tracking, or non-system fonts are used.
- The visual reads as a poster from two meters or at phone-timeline scale.
- The fighter supplied the exact path and a one-sentence shipping claim.

**DQ:** missing/unreadable artifact, wrong-size artboard, or network asset.
