# Brief lane stock pack

**Clock:** 20 minutes  
**Tiebreak:** clearer hierarchy

## Sealed task

Using the three source excerpts included by the referee, write one
`decision-brief.md` answering: “Should a 12-person product team replace its
weekly status meeting with asynchronous updates?” Give a recommendation,
three source-linked findings, the strongest counterargument, and a reversible
30-day trial. Do not introduce facts not present in the sealed sources.

## Rubric

- The declared Markdown file exists, opens, and contains no embedded network asset.
- A direct recommendation appears before supporting detail.
- Three findings each cite one of the supplied source labels.
- The strongest counterargument is represented fairly.
- The 30-day trial has an owner, cadence, success measure, and rollback trigger.
- Unsupported claims are absent.
- The fighter supplied the exact path and a one-sentence shipping claim.

**DQ:** missing/unreadable artifact or fabricated source material.
